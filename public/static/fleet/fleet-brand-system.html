04

Fleet Rules

Non-negotiable across all brands

The rules that make the family look like a family.

Warning

No sub-brand mark is production-final until name + trademark clearance closes (Section 3 of the Verification Packet). These are draft silhouettes.

Warning

Regulated brands (MHI, MCR, MMOS, MFS, MFW, MELA) require compliance-packet closure before any public mark, tagline, or claim appears anywhere — including social handles.

Shape

Every mark lives inside the master shield silhouette. No square, circle, or wordmark-only exceptions without approval.

Type

Playfair Display · headlines. Inter · body. IBM Plex Mono · metadata & tags. Zero exceptions across all 10 brands.

Neutrals

Navy #0A1628 for authority, Ivory #F7F3EA for paper. Every brand uses these — the accent color is the only variable.

Accent Use

Accent color = fill of the mark, headline emphasis (italic `em`), and CTA background. Never for body copy.

Voice

All brands ship in the same voice family: straight-shooting, specific, values-anchored — with sector-appropriate vocabulary.

Naming

Sub-brand always reads "McKnight [Brand]" in the wordmark — never "[Brand] by McKnight." Parent first. Exception: The Contracting Preacher (Dr. McKnight override).
