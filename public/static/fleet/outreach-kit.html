The Cold Open

30-60 seconds maxDirect dial
First words
"Hi [First Name], this is Dr. McKnight with The Contracting Preacher. Did I catch you at a bad time?" [Pause. Let them answer.] If they say "yes, bad time" → "Understood. When's a better window — later today or is tomorrow morning cleaner?" If they say "what's this about?" → "Fair question. Straight up: I help small businesses in [State] win federal contracts. Saw [Company] and wanted to ask if that's something you're pursuing right now — or if it's on the list but hasn't been figured out yet." [Pause again. Their answer decides the next 60 seconds.]

Objection Handling

The 5 objections you'll hear · every single time
Objection · 01

"We already work with a consultant."

"Great — that means you already understand the value. Two quick questions: (1) how's their win rate been? (2) do they only bid when you can win, or do they bid whenever the deadline is open? If those two answers make you happy, I'll leave you alone. If not, worth a 15-min second opinion."
Objection · 02

"It's not the right time."

"Understood. When you say not the right time — is it a bandwidth thing, a cash-flow thing, or is federal not on the horizon at all? Different situations, different answers. I don't want to push if it's genuinely not fit — but if it's bandwidth, that's exactly what I solve."
Objection · 03

"Send me some info."

"Happy to. So the info actually helps you — one quick question first: what's the specific outcome you'd want from federal contracts in the next 12 months? That way I send something relevant instead of a brochure that sits in your inbox."
Objection · 04

"How much does it cost?"

"Fair question. Depends entirely on scope, but ballpark: our Foundation package (SAM + first cert + capability statement) is around $2,850. Full pursuit with a proposal is roughly $4,500. Ongoing retainer is $1,850 a month. Any of those feel wildly outside your range?"
Objection · 05

"Can you guarantee we'll win a contract?"

"No — and be careful of anyone who does. What I guarantee is: I only bid when you can win. I document the process. I show you the compliance matrix before we submit. If we don't win, you have a clear reason why and a stronger position for the next one. That's the honest answer."
