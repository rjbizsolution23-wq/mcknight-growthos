Client Handout · v1.0SAM.gov Registration

Everything you need — before you register.

SAM.gov registration is the front door to federal contracting. This is the exact list of what to have ready before you start. Print it. Gather everything. Then we register together.

Business Identity

  • Legal business nameExactly as on your formation documents
  • DBA / trade name(s)Any names you also do business under
  • Physical addressNo P.O. boxes — must be verifiable
  • Mailing addressIf different from physical
  • Entity typeLLC · Corp · Nonprofit · Sole Prop
  • State of incorporation+ formation date
  • Fiscal-year endMonth + day

Financial & Tax

  • EIN (Employer ID Number)From your IRS SS-4 or CP-575 letter
  • Bank routing + account #For EFT payments (kept private)
  • ACH contact infoBank name + phone

Business Details

  • NAICS codesPrimary + secondary (up to 5)
  • PSC / FSC codesProduct/Service classifications
  • Business typesSmall biz · veteran · woman-owned · etc.
  • Corporate structureForeign ownership disclosures
  • Website URLPublic-facing business site
  • Number of employeesFull and part-time totals
  • Annual revenueLast 3 years averaged

Points of Contact

  • Entity AdministratorThe person who manages the SAM account
  • Electronic Business POCContact for procurement
  • Government Business POCContact for federal buyers
  • Past Performance POCReferences contact
⚠ Never share these with anyone (including us) in email or chat: SAM login credentials · MFA codes · full SSN · full bank account numbers. We only work with the AOR / owner directly through secure channels for sensitive fields.
