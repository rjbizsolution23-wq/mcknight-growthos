South Carolina Small Business Wins $2.1M USDA Federal Contract
Torres Logistics Group joins the 500+ businesses coached to federal contract awards by Dr. McKnight — The Contracting Preacher.

Columbia, SC — July 23, 2026 — Torres Logistics Group, a Columbia-based 8(a)-certified small business, has been awarded a $2.1 million federal contract by the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) to provide facility maintenance services across the Southeast region. The multi-year contract is expected to create 18 new jobs across Columbia, Charleston, and Aiken.

The contract is the culmination of an 18-month federal readiness engagement led by Dr. McKnight — The Contracting Preacher, a Columbia-based federal contracting consultancy that has helped over 500 small businesses nationwide navigate SAM.gov registration, SBA certification, and proposal preparation to win federal contracts.

"Torres Logistics is exactly what the federal marketplace was designed for — a well-run, values-driven small business that just needed a clear path in. Angela showed up prepared. She followed the process. This contract is the fruit of that discipline." — Dr. McKnight, Federal Contracting Consultant

Dr. McKnight's practice, which maintains an 89% proposal win rate — more than double the industry standard — has helped clients win a collective $50M+ in federal contracts. The practice specializes in end-to-end federal contracting support, including SAM.gov registration, 8(a) / HUBZone / WOSB / SDVOSB certification, bid and proposal writing, and post-award compliance.

"Dr. McKnight made what felt like an impossible process feel simple. He walked with us the entire way — from SAM registration to signed contract. Our team is now positioned for federal growth we never thought possible." — Angela Torres, President, Torres Logistics Group

Torres Logistics begins performance under the contract on August 1, 2026.
