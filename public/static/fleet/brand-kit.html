Voice & Messaging

Faith · Federal · Faithful
Do

Sound like this

  • Straight-shooting: "We only bid when you can win."
  • Faith-anchored, never preachy: verse below the fold, not the headline.
  • Specific over abstract: "$50M+ won," not "millions won."
  • Warm authority: mentor tone, not sales tone.
  • Federal-fluent: use SAM.gov, 8(a), NAICS, set-aside, RFP correctly.
Don't

Never sound like this

  • Hype-marketing: no "SECRETS THEY DON'T WANT YOU TO KNOW."
  • Prosperity-pitch: faith is stewardship, not a payout.
  • Government jargon dumped without translation.
  • Generic small-business coaching cliché.
  • Emoji-heavy or overly casual — this is a fiduciary voice.
