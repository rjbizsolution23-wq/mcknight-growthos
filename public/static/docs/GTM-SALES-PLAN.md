## 5. Pricing Strategy

- **Anchor high, sell the middle**: present Empire first, close on Growth Command. B should feel like the obvious deal.
- **Setup fees fund CAC**; MRR is the business. Target blend: **$250 avg MRR/client**.
- **Annual option**: 2 months free (10 months' price) — improves cash + retention.
- **Tax-season / industry promos**: "Tax Command Center — live before Jan 15" at Launch pricing with 6-month minimum.
- **Never discount MRR; discount setup instead.** MRR is the valuation of this business.
- **Cost floor reality**: Cloudflare edge ≈ ~$0–5/client/mo → **gross margins 90%+** on software; your only real costs are sales time and onboarding labor (~2 hrs/client with the Change Agent doing the heavy lifting).

## 6. The Sales System

**Philosophy: sell it the way it works — use GrowthOS to sell GrowthOS.** Every prospect experience IS the product demo.

### The Pipeline (build it in ClientOS — 8 pipelines already exist)

```
LEAD → PERSONALIZED DEMO SENT → DEMO CALL BOOKED → CALL HELD → PROPOSAL → CLOSED → ONBOARDED (48h) → 90-DAY REVIEW → UPSELL/REFERRAL
```

### The Killer Move: The 60-Second Personalized Demo
Because every funnel takes URL params, you can send any prospect **their own funnel with their name/brand/colors already on it** — before they ever pay:

```
https://mcknight-growthos.pages.dev/t/tax-lead?seoTitle=Johnson%20Tax%20Group&brandColor=%232F6B4A&cta=Book%20My%20Free%20Tax%20Review&ghlTag=demo-johnson
```

**Outreach flow:**
1. Find prospect (their industry = one of the 42 slugs)
2. Build their demo link in 60 seconds (or via /builder)
3. Send: *"I already built your new lead system. Here it is with your name on it: [link]. Want the keys?"*
4. Demo call → live Change Agent moment (see §11) → close.

This turns cold outreach reply rates from ~1% to 8–15% because the "attachment" is their own live site.

### Sales Cadence (per prospect)
- Day 0: personalized demo link (email + SMS if possible)
- Day 1: follow-up with a 90-sec Loom walking through THEIR demo
- Day 3: case study / social proof touch
- Day 5: "The demo link expires Friday" (urgency — then actually 404 it via param change)
- Day 7: breakup email ("Should I close your build slot?") — highest reply rate of the sequence

## 11. Demo Playbook

**The 15-minute demo call structure — the Change Agent is the closer:**

1. **(2 min) Their demo funnel** — share screen on THEIR personalized link. "This exists right now. It's yours."
2. **(3 min) THE MOMENT** — open the Change Agent and type, live, while they watch:
   - *"make it feel more premium and luxurious"* → fonts/shadows/effects transform
   - *"add falling snow and make the button glow"* → instant
   - Then their request: "What would YOU change? Say it in plain English." → type their exact words → it happens. **This closes the deal.**
3. **(3 min) Command Center** — leads arriving, pipeline, one-click CSV, analytics
4. **(2 min) The stack math** — cross out their current tools one by one, total the savings
5. **(2 min) Guarantee + close** — 48 hours or refund. "Which funnel do we deploy first?"
6. **(3 min) Objections + payment link** (Stripe checkout is built in — send `/api/checkout` link live on the call)

**Backup theater:** if AI keys ever fail mid-demo, fall back to URL params in the address bar — visually identical magic ("watch what happens when I change this word in the URL").
